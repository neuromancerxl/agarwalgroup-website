Sai Dutt Kotrakona

2026–present

I received my M.Sc. in Nanoscience from the University of Groningen and my B.Tech. in Metallurgical Engineering from IIT (BHU), Varanasi. Previously, I worked on ferroelectric hafnia thin films & devices at IISc Bangalore. During my master’s research with Prof. Beatriz Noheda, I studied pulse-driven resistive dynamics in samarium nickelate thin films for neuromorphic applications through experimental and modeling means. My research interests include the growth and characterization of low-dimensional functional materials, particularly how crystal structure and phase transitions influence their electronic and optical properties. Outside the lab, I enjoy reading science fiction and history.
